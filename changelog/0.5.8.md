When an agent wants to change something, it asks in a card where you type, and shows exactly what will happen.

## Improved

- The card names the action, the command or the files, then Deny (Esc) or Allow once (↩).
- Allowing for the rest of the chat, or stopping the agent, is behind "…".
- Otter Mail moved to mail.otterware.app.

Turn a bug report or feature idea into a clear GitHub issue, with help from your own coding agent.

## New

- "Send feedback" in the mailbox menu, Settings, and ⌘K.
- Choose a bug report or feature request, and the affected platform.
- Add a screenshot and anonymous diagnostics; review everything before opening GitHub.
- On the Mac, Claude or Codex investigates and brings its draft back into the app.
- Use Ghostty or your usual terminal; Otter Mail remembers your choice.

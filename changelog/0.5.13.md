---
title: Safer reruns
date: 2026-09-30
---

Running the setup or the tour again leaves your mail and settings as they were.

## Fixed

- Running the setup again no longer switches your agent when the saved one is offline.
- The tour opens a conversation you've already read, so nothing gets marked read.
- The update card's progress bar shows its full track in dark mode.
- A failed update no longer offers "What's new", and its message fits on one line.
